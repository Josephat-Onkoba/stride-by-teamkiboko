Why is the slider locked at 90 g/h?

This is a hard biological limit based on intestinal transporter saturation. The SGLT1 transporter can absorb glucose at ~60 g/h, and GLUT5 can absorb fructose at ~30 g/h. Exceeding 90 g/h will pool in the gut and cause severe gastrointestinal distress.
